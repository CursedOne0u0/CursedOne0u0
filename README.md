I build small browser games to play with friends: local multiplayer over LAN, no installs, no accounts, just open and play.

## Games

**[local-browser-games](https://github.com/CursedOne0u0/local-browser-games)**, five games and counting:

- **Neon Pong Showdown**: 2P LAN Pong with power-ups, or solo vs AI
- **Tank Duel**: 2P LAN maze tanks with bouncing shells and weapon pickups
- **Bomb Tag**: 2P LAN hot potato with dash escapes
- **Breakout Blast**: solo brick-breaker
- **Pong Duo**: solo vs AI or 2P on one keyboard

## Stack

Python (stdlib-only servers), vanilla JS + Canvas, Linux (KDE Plasma, kitty)
